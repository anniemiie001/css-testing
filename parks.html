Neighborhood Walks

Walking through the local neighborhoods reveals blooming magnolia trees, tulips, and daffodils popping up through the last patches of melted snow.

Early morning walks are the quietest, offering fresh air and plenty of interesting scents for a curious dog to explore along the sidewalks.
